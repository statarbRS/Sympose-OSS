# Demo guide

Start at the [hosted application](https://ethical-opera-murphy-somewhere.trycloudflare.com) and use the
prominent synthetic role entries. The [guided walkthrough](https://ethical-opera-murphy-somewhere.trycloudflare.com/walkthrough)
contains direct links for each area.

Recommended path:

1. Open the organizer workspace and inspect the attention-first event overview.
2. Open CFP to see form configuration, submissions, decision evidence, and notifications.
3. Open Review to inspect round setup, reviewer access, conflicts, scorecards, and organizer results.
4. Open Speaker Operations and Content Library to inspect tasks, messages, uploaded versions, comments,
   approvals, and downloads.
5. Open Program to inspect schedule placement and conflicts.
6. Open Publication to compare draft state, approvals, sealed releases, and drift.
7. Open the public program, agenda, sessions, speakers, gallery, and itinerary.
8. Return to Readiness, Delivery, CRM, and person history for the operational overview.

The demo is synthetic and safe to explore. Do not enter real participant data or provider credentials.
The exact deployed candidate is shown by `/health`; a valid release returns a 40-character build SHA.
